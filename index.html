About

Cities transformed, together

Not competition. Not isolation. Churches and ministries working together, in unity, for the good of their city.

Our Mission

Transform cities through unity

The mission of City Church Project is to transform cities by cultivating unity in the Body of Christ through intentional equipping, mobilization, and shared Kingdom purpose.

Our Vision

Every person, whole in Jesus

We envision cities transformed as we empower individuals and ministries to embrace their God-given assignment, working together in unity to build networks that enrich communities, eradicate poverty, restore dignity, and lead every person into the wholeness only found in Jesus Christ.

What We Do

Empowering the Church, four ways

The Church is stronger when we work together.

Empowering the Church through Fiscal Sponsorship

Turn God-given vision into meaningful action

City Church Project equips and empowers the Church to turn God-given vision into meaningful action. Through practical resources, administrative support, and collaborative partnerships, we help leaders launch and strengthen ministries, overcome barriers, and bring lasting transformation to their communities.

We believe the Church is stronger when we work together, steward our resources well, and unite around the work God has called us to do.

Empowering the Church through Shared Kingdom Purpose

Across boundaries, toward one purpose

City Church Project brings churches and ministry leaders together across denominational, cultural, and geographic boundaries to pursue a shared Kingdom purpose. Through networking, strategic partnerships, and collaborative gatherings, we equip leaders with shared wisdom, mobilize collective resources, and cultivate relationships rooted in unity rather than competition.

Together, we can accomplish far more than we ever could alone and create lasting transformation in our communities.

Empowering the Church through Supportive Services

Practical tools for lasting ministries

City Church Project strengthens churches and ministries by providing practical resources that support their growth, effectiveness, and long-term sustainability. Through future grant opportunities, funding support, data and community needs assessments, and program evaluation, we will equip leaders with the tools and insight they need to thrive.

By combining shared resources with strategic collaboration, we help ministries make informed decisions, maximize their impact, and build a stronger, more sustainable Church.

Empowering the Church through Biblical Teaching

Truth connected to practical action

City Church Project provides biblical teaching that connects spiritual truth with practical action. Through teaching, training, and shared learning experiences, we equip leaders to grow in God's Word, mobilize believers to live out their faith, and foster unity across the Body of Christ.

Our goal is to cultivate Kingdom-minded leaders who put biblical principles into practice to transform their communities.

Who We Are

Meet Jamie Lindsay

As an author, speaker, and community advocate, Jamie Lindsay is compelled by the Father's love for people. Her passion is empowering the local church to tangibly express God's love and effectively share the gospel to those in need through their radical giving and practical acts of service.

As founder of City Church Project, Jamie's mission is to equip churches so that believers are empowered and mobilized to meet every need in their community. Jamie's unique perspective combines working as an outreach director and continuous improvement coach with her background in public health to bring a unique approach to developing church outreach programs.

As the Director of Outreach at her local church, Jamie and her team work with individuals who are experiencing financial difficulties or facing homelessness. She has also helped develop relationships with a local elementary school to develop a mentorship program for at-risk children. Additionally, she has helped her church connect with a local neighborhood, where they work to understand the needs of the residents and provide programming to support those needs as well as invest in families. She has worked to build bridges between their work in the neighborhood and local resources and employers to better help those living in the community.

As a corporate project manager and continuous improvement coach, Jamie has worked with senior leaders and managers to improve business operations and implement large-scale changes. She has also led several statewide healthcare improvement projects in her home state of Michigan. It is this experience and knowledge that has positioned her to be a voice to the church so that believers are mobilized to build the Kingdom in their communities. Jamie holds a bachelor's degree in public health.

When not changing the world one city at a time, Jamie and her family enjoy spending time outdoors kayaking, biking, and just relaxing at the beach.

Author & Speaker Director of Outreach Public Health, B.A. Continuous Improvement Coach Michigan
